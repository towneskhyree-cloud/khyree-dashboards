01 · The Requirement

To operate a business in DeKalb County, you need two things.

A

Certificate of Occupancy

Permission for the space to be used for business.

↗DeKalb County — Certificate of OccupancyOpen
B

Business License

Permission for you to do business in the county.

↗DeKalb County — Business LicenseOpen
Simple version:The C.O. covers the building. The business license covers the person. You need both — and Cut Creaters already handles one of them for you.
02 · What You Need At Cut Creaters

We cover the building. You cover your license.

✓

C.O. — covered by us

Braiders, locticians, hair stylists, barbers, nail techs, lash techs and makeup artists do not need their own C.O. You operate under the Cut Creaters Certificate of Occupancy.

Other services may also be allowed under our C.O. — reviewed case by case.

!

Your license — required

Every suite owner must carry their own business license.

Nail techs, lash techs and estheticians must also hold a current Georgia State Board license.

03 · Getting Your Business License

Two steps. Both can be done online.

A

Register with the Secretary of State

Get your Federal EIN
