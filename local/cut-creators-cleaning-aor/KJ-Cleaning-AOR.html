1 Doors — Open & Close

  • 8:00 AM — Unlock and open the front door. Lights on in the hallway. Quick walk-through to make sure the building is ready for the day.
  • 8:00 PM — Close and lock the front door. Hallway lights off. Confirm the door is actually locked before you leave.

2 Hallways — Every Day (the whole suite, end to end)

  • Sweep the full length of the hallway — both directions, corners included.
  • Mop the hallway floor (spot-mop spills immediately, full mop daily).
  • Empty every hallway trash can and put in fresh liners.
  • Wipe down door handles, light switches, and hallway rails.
  • Clean the glass on the front door and any hallway glass — no smudges.
  • Spot-clean scuffs and marks off the hallway walls and baseboards.
  • Dust ledges, vents, and frames along the hallway.
  • Report anything broken (lights out, leaks, damage) the same day.
What's NOT yours: You do not clean inside the individual rooms/suites — each operator handles their own room. Your lane is the hallways, the front door, and the outside front.

3 Outside Front — Every Day

  • Sweep the entrance and sidewalk directly in front of the building.
  • Pick up any trash, cups, or debris around the front — morning and before close.
  • Shake out / straighten the entry mats.
  • Make sure the entrance is safe and clear (no ice, no blockage, nothing in the walkway).

4 How You Get Paid

Weekly Rate $250 / week
Pay is simple: the list is the job. Every item on this sheet, done Monday through Saturday, checked off on the weekly grid below. At the end of the week, we walk it together — list complete = $250 paid. Items skipped or half-done get fixed before pay goes out. No surprises, no guessing — everything you're responsible for is on this one page.
