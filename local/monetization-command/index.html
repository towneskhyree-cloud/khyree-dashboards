The
Money Map.

Every way Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube can put money in your pocket — plus the deals, products and bookings that pay you directly. For each one: what it pays you for, what unlocks it, how to switch it on, and exactly how the money hits your bank.

Bucket 1 · Platform Programs

The apps pay you

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube each have built-in money programs. You meet a bar (usually a follower count), flip it on in the app, and they pay you monthly to a bank account you hook up once.

Bucket 2 · Your Own Rails

You get paid directly

Brand deals, affiliate links, your products, and bookings. No follower bar, no waiting on the platform. This is where the real money is for a barber/brand — it pays straight to your bank or Stripe.

Straight up: I can't log into your Instagram/TikTok/bank and click the buttons for you — those need your login, your ID, and your bank info. What this page does is show you every door, in order, with the exact menu to tap so you can turn them on in minutes. Follower bars and payout minimums shift often, so anywhere you see VERIFY, trust what your own app's Monetization screen says over any number here.

The Programs

Tap a platform to focus. Each card = one way that app can pay you. Green tag = easy to unlock, orange = mid, pink = needs size, lime = a deal/direct channel with no hard bar.

How the money lands

All those programs above dump into just a few "pipes." Set these up once and every program on that platform pays into it. Do this first — if you earn before a payout account exists, some platforms hold or even forfeit the money.

Your ladder

Don't try to switch on everything at once. This is the order that makes sense — start at the top no matter your size, then unlock the next rung as your following grows.
