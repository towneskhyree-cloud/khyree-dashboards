## Deen check (not a ruling, ask someone qualified)
- Vanguard cash brokerage account itself: generally fine. Avoid margin.
- NVDA / AMD / ORCL: need sharia screening (business activity, debt and interest-income ratios). Common screens often pass chip names, Oracle is tighter. Check, don't assume.
- QQQ, VOO, SPY, SMH: broad ETFs hold banks, alcohol, entertainment, etc. Most scholars say these are not compliant. Halal-screened ETFs exist (HLAL, SPUS, SPTE) if you want the ETF route.
- His whole frame is "get rich". That is not the frame. Wealth is rizq; halal first.
