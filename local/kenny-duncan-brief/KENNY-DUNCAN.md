# Kenny Duncan — The Deep Dive

*Philadelphia master barber · celebrity groomer · Andis North American Artistic Team Lead · founder of Barbers Who Care*

*Compiled August 13, 2026. Every claim is sourced; links at the end of each section.*

---

## The One-Paragraph Version

Kenny Duncan (b. 1981, raised in West Philadelphia) is one of the most decorated barbers in America. Since 2003 he has co-owned the 14-chair **Main Attraction Unisex Salon** at 5610 Lancaster Ave in West Philly, and from that chair he built a career that runs through four **Lady Gaga** world tours, the **Adele Live** world tour, Michael B. Jordan's looks in ***Creed***, ***Creed II***, and ***Fantastic Four***, and the locker room of the **Philadelphia 76ers**. He is the **North American Artistic Team Lead for Andis** (the oldest clipper maker in America), has his own limited-edition Andis clipper, sells the **K Duncan Collection** of grooming tools, and since 2013 has run the nonprofit **Barbers Who Care**, which has given thousands of free back-to-school haircuts to Philly kids. His motto: **"Be Great On Purpose."**

## 1. Early Life & The Why

- Born **1981**, raised in **West Philadelphia**. His mother pushed him toward barbering early.
- His origin story is about confidence, not hair: *"Growing up with low self-esteem, a haircut gave me nine points of confidence. It helped me feel good about myself, which allowed me to be present in the moment for whatever it is I'm doing."*
- That idea — grooming as confidence — is the through-line of everything he's built: the celebrity work, the education brand, and the nonprofit.
- Behind the chair, he starts every cut with two questions: **"How was your week?"** and **"What was the most productive part about it?"** — "letting his mouth run as much as his razor" (6abc).

## 4. Andis: 20 Years and Counting

- Joined Andis as an educator/platform artist in **February 2005**; rose to **U.S. Lead Educator**, then in **2021** was named **North American Artistic Team Lead** of Andis's new Global Artistic Team (alongside UK barber Kevin Luchmun as International lead). He develops education programs for barbers across the US and Canada and gives artistic direction to Andis creative projects.
- **Andis Educator of the Year, 2011 and 2012**; Andis 10-years-of-service award (~2015); Xotics "2014 Educator of the Year."
- **Andis Creator Series — "Confidence Built Through Hair"**: his signature collection of five haircut tutorials across four curl patterns (The Urban Crop, The Tapered Curly Cut, etc.).
- Teaches at major shows: America's Beauty Show (Chicago), IBS New York, Bronner Bros. (past competition winner, later emcee of the Andis Total Look contest), BarberEVO Atlanta.

Sources: [Behind the Chair — Global Artistic Team](https://behindthechair.com/news/andis-appoints-kevin-luchmun-kenny-duncan-to-lead-new-global-artistic-team/) · [Andis educator profile](https://andis.com/BarberStylistEducation/EducatorsDetail/?EducatorID=7) · [Andis Creator Series](https://andis.com/Community/CreatorSeries/KennyDuncan) · [kennyduncan.com/accomplishments](http://kennyduncan.com/accomplishments/) · [Creator Series playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwFhYoamRzEnyTC-LoLRda-GU9beEsmmc)

## 7. Barbers Who Care (2013– )

- Nonprofit he founded in **2013** "with the goal of raising the self-esteem of the youth through grooming."
- Annual **back-to-school giveback**: ~25–30 Philly-area and celebrity barbers give free haircuts plus school supplies, backpacks, sneakers, and manicures — mostly at the West Philadelphia YMCA, for kids 5–17.
- Cumulative impact: **3,000+ kids / ~4,000 free cuts** over 15 years. The 15th annual event (Aug 25, 2024) served **403 children**, with former Eagle Rodney McLeod's Change Our Future Foundation providing 600 book bags.
- Why grade-schoolers: *"We select grade schools because those are formidable years for kids where self esteem and self confidence are built."*

Sources: [Modern Salon](https://www.modernsalon.com/1079139/barbers-who-care-have-big-hearts) · [Inquirer 2024](https://www.inquirer.com/news/philadelphia/philadelphia-free-haircuts-back-to-school-barbers-who-care-20240825.html) · [FOX29](https://www.fox29.com/news/celebrity-barbers-continue-annual-free-haircut-event-in-west-philly-ahead-of-back-to-school) · [Change Our Future press release](https://changeourfuture.org/press-release-former-eagle-rodney-mcleod-erika-mcleod-join-barbers-who-care-sunday-to-give-kids-free-haircuts-school-supplies-sneakers-and-more/)

## 8. Philosophy: "Be Great On Purpose"

His signature framework — a talk walks through "the four elements of the blueprint to greatness," and the blueprint motif became the artwork on his Envy II clipper. The core message across every interview: greatness is deliberate, confidence is buildable, and the chair is a platform for both.

Source: [Be Great on Purpose — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2zZAqM-NAe4)
