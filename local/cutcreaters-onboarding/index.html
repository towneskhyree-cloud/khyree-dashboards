Licensing & Compliance

Where Georgia stands right now for natural hair — and why Cut Creaters holds a standard either way.

✅ Where it stands today

As of right now, natural hairstylists, locticians, and braiders do not have to hold a state cosmetology license to operate in Georgia. You can work without one today.

⏳ What's coming

Georgia is moving toward some form of certification requirement for natural hairstyles going forward. It's still in talks with the politicians — not final. But we'd rather give you the basis now and be ready than get caught flat when it lands.

🏛️ Why we hold the standard anyway

License or no license, Cut Creaters follows what the state, city, and county require of us. That's the point: if we follow it, we can ask everyone else in the house to follow it too.

We don't want to be known as cappers — saying one thing and doing another. We hold our act together first. Then the standard means something.

What that means for you
  • You can start now — no state license needed today for natural hair.
  • Keep any training or certification you do have; put it on file.
  • Follow the house standards (sanitation, conduct) — that's non-negotiable.
  • When GA sets the certification rule, we help you get ahead of it, not scramble.
📌Always verify current rules. Requirements change. Confirm the latest with the Georgia State Board of Cosmetology & Barbers and your city/county before relying on any of this. This hub reflects our current understanding, not legal advice.
