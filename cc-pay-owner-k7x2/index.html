One-time setup (Khyree, ~15 min in Square)

1
Confirm PNC is the deposit account. Square → Settings → Bank Accounts.
2
Turn on ACH bank payments. Square → Settings → Payments → enable ACH (1% fee — much cheaper than card).
3
Create one Subscription Plan per rent tier. Square → Payments → Subscriptions → Create plan: "Suite Rent $175/wk" → weekly billing. Repeat for each tier ($150, $175, $200, $250…). Square gives each plan a checkout link.
4
Give Claude the plan links → they get pasted into the signup page so tenants flow straight from signup → Square bank setup with zero waiting.

Per tenant (the whole job after setup)

1
They sign up on our page → team gets a phone ping instantly.
2
They land on Square's secure page and link their bank themselves. We NEVER touch or type anyone's bank numbers. Bank verifies once (instant login or 1–2 day micro-deposits).
3
Done forever. Friday pull · auto-receipt to them · payment visible to us · money in PNC.
If someone's bank is slow to verify and we want them live THIS Friday → put that one on card on file instead (instant, slightly higher fee), switch to bank later. · Truth about timing: the pull happens every Friday, but Square picks the exact hour (batch processing) — "Friday" is guaranteed, "6:00 PM sharp" is not.

Protection — every party covered

✓
Tenant: written authorization they check themselves · cancel anytime with notice · receipt on every charge · bank info only ever entered on Square's secure page.
✓
Cut Creaters: signed consent on record · automatic Friday collection · every payment logged in Square · deposits traceable into PNC — the clean money trail the business never had.
✓
The team: instant ping on every signup · everything visible in one Square login · no cash, no chasing, no he-said-she-said.
Rule of the machine: nobody pays through RentRoom, nobody pays cash for weekly rent once enrolled. One rail: our page → Square → PNC.
