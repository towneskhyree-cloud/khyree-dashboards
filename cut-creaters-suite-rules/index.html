Your Room, Your BusinessHere's What You Can Touch

You rent the room. You run your own business inside it. But the building is licensed, inspected, and insured as one property — so a few things are yours to change, a few need a yes in writing, and a few can never happen. This page is the whole list.

✓ Yours

Do it. No permission needed. If it's freestanding, removable, and stays inside your four walls, it's your call.

◇ Ask first

Might be a yes — but it needs written approval before you start. Verbal doesn't count, and neither does "someone said it was fine."

✕ Never

Not a preference. These are fire code, county code, the building's lease, or the insurance policy. They fail inspections and they end agreements.
