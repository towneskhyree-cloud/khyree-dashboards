Read This Before You Set It

4 NOTES

Why $249 and not $199

$199 puts you inside the ring-light shelf, where you get compared on lumens and lose. $249 puts you above it, where the arch shape, the gold finish, and the built-in mount are the reason — not the spec sheet.

You can always run $199 as a promo. You can't quietly raise $199 to $249 later.

The cost numbers are estimates

The BOM above is built from comparable part pricing, not your invoices. Drop your real numbers into the fields and the whole page re-reads.

Watch the finish and labor lines especially — hand-painting and assembly are where small-batch products quietly lose their margin.

If you ever go wholesale

Shops will want it at roughly half of retail. At $249 that's a $124 wholesale price against a cost — a unit, which is too thin to be worth the boxes.

If wholesale is the plan, the MSRP has to start at $299–$349.

What raises the ceiling

Three things move this from $249 to $349 without changing the hardware: photography that shows it lit in a real chair, a named colorway (Gold is already one), and a warranty line people can read.

The build is already premium. The presentation has to match it.
